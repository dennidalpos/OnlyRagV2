## Concrete policy choice — awaiting decision

1. **AGPL route:** prepare an AGPL-compliant distribution proposal covering the applicable combined work, full terms/notices and exact corresponding source with build instructions. Review Electron/Sidecar boundaries and NVIDIA terms before selecting the final redistribution scope. Keep the original MIT permission for project source where applicable; do not assume it waives dependency obligations. The complete GNU license page could not be retrieved during this review, so no clause-by-clause legal certification is claimed.
2. **Commercial route:** document the Artifex grant applicable to this application, component versions, redistribution recipients and scope. Verify rights privately without copying credentials or confidential agreement text into the repository. This route cannot be certified merely by selecting it; purchasing or accepting an agreement is outside this task's authorization.

Both routes require fixing the observed notice gaps, verifying exact frozen dependency provenance, and reviewing distribution terms. Choosing a route authorizes preparation of concrete notices/build changes for review; it does not authorize publication, a purchase, relicensing third-party components or deletion of existing evidence. If neither route can be established, retain the P1 and withhold any claim of public distribution readiness.
