## Concrete policy choice — AGPL evaluation selected

The owner selected **evaluation of an AGPL-compliant distribution**. The [prepared proposal](./distribution-agpl-proposal.md) treats the combined application as covered, preserves original component grants, proposes isolated CPU release packaging, and specifies corresponding-source delivery under section 6(d). [Draft notices and seven full text/attribution files](./licenses/agpl-route-2026-10-10/NOTICE-DRAFT.md) are retained with source URLs/hashes. Complete AGPL terms were reviewed from official Artifex MuPDF `COPYING` and pinned to an immutable revision after GNU's endpoint timed out; no exact GNU endpoint byte identity or bundled MuPDF source-revision match is claimed.

Read-only follow-up [evidence](./licenses/agpl-route-2026-10-10/evidence.json) binds **66 wordfreq 3.1.1 data files**, **20 NVIDIA DLLs** and **five ONNX Runtime GPU native files** to installed-distribution bytes. It records installed RECORD/terms hashes and the proposed CPU wheel's official URL/hash. Independent upstream provenance and final artifact/source correspondence remain open. Exact-release PyPI credits are retained instead of mislabeling the current upstream wordfreq NOTICE as a release file. The installed cuDNN January 2020 supplement/version discrepancy, missing exact font copyrights and incomplete native/transitive notices are explicit residues.

The commercial route is deferred, not selected or disproved. Preparation does not authorize publication, a purchase, overriding third-party terms or deleting retained artifacts. The CPU proposal changes shipped OCR acceleration and therefore requires review before changing dependency/build contracts. The old frozen inventory remains historical evidence; prepared notices are not yet installed. No legal/release certification is claimed from this phase.
