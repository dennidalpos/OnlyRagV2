The installed cuDNN January 2020 supplement/version discrepancy, missing exact font copyrights and incomplete native/transitive notices are explicit residues.

The commercial route is deferred, not selected or disproved. Preparation does not authorize publication, a purchase, overriding third-party terms or deleting retained artifacts. The old frozen inventory remains historical evidence; prepared notices are not yet installed. No legal/release certification is claimed from this phase.
